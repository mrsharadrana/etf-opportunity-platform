# ETF Decision Platform Roadmap

## Vision

Build a rules-based ETF Decision Engine that tells investors:

- What ETF to BUY
- What ETF to HOLD
- What ETF to REDUCE
- What ETF to EXIT
- How much capital to DEPLOY
- Where to DEPLOY it
- Initial Stop Loss
- Trailing Stop Loss
- Market Overview
- Market Rotation

Orders are executed manually via Zerodha Kite.
