# ETF Decision Platform

## Problem

Investors struggle to answer:

- What should I buy?
- What should I hold?
- What should I reduce?
- What should I exit?
- How much capital should I deploy?

Most decisions are driven by emotions, news, and social media.

---

## Solution

A rules-based ETF Decision Engine that generates investment decisions using:

- Momentum
- Market Regime
- Fear Analysis
- Risk Management
- Market Rotation

---

## Investment Style

- Quant Investing
- Weekly Decisions
- Monthly Decisions
- Low Turnover
- Trend Following
- Capital Preservation First
- Wealth Creation Second

---

## User Input

Opportunity Buffer

Examples:

- ₹1 Lakh
- ₹5 Lakh
- ₹10 Lakh
- ₹25 Lakh

---

## Platform Output

### Signals

- STRONG_BUY
- BUY
- HOLD
- REDUCE_20
- REDUCE_40
- EXIT

### Deployment

- Deploy Amount
- Allocation Plan

### Risk

- Initial Stop Loss
- Trailing Stop Loss

### Market Intelligence

- Market Overview
- Market Rotation
