# ETF Opportunity Allocation Platform

## Vision

A rules-based ETF investing platform focused on:

* Trend Following
* Momentum Investing
* Global Rotation
* Crash Deployment
* Capital Preservation
* Opportunity-Based Allocation

Target:

* 20–25 decisions per year
* No daily trading
* No stock picking
* ETF-focused investing

---

## Core Engines

### Data Engine

* ETF price history
* Indicators
* Historical analytics

### Market Regime Engine

* Bull
* Correction
* Panic
* Recovery
* Euphoria

### Fear & Greed Engine

* Fear score (0–100)
* Opportunity detection

### Opportunity Score Engine

* Trend
* Momentum
* Relative Strength
* Regime
* Fear

### Crash Layer Engine

* Layer 1 = 10%
* Layer 2 = 20%
* Layer 3 = 20%
* Layer 4 = 20%
* Layer 5 = 30%

### Portfolio Allocation Engine

* ETF ranking
* Capital allocation
* Portfolio recommendations

### Rotation Engine

* Cross-asset rotation
* International allocation
* Defensive allocation

### Backtesting Engine

* Historical simulation
* CAGR
* Drawdown
* Sharpe Ratio
* Trade frequency
